Airsens Isokinetic Monitor

Version 1.1. Calculates the isokinetic flow velocity percentage, the ratio of sampling nozzle velocity to stack fluid velocity, and judges it against stack-sampling standards.

Method

Ve = Qs ÷ (π Ds²/4). The probe flow is corrected from meter to stack conditions with the ideal gas law, then Vf = Qf,s ÷ (π dn²/4). I = Vf/Ve × 100. EPA Method 5 accepts 90–110%.

Assumptions

Stack flow is entered at actual stack conditions. Moisture, gas molecular weight and pitot readings are not used. Real-time mode is simulated until sensors are connected.
